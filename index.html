Why the page is the interface

No form, no preview

Half the invoice tools in the world make you fill a form and squint at a small preview. Here the A4 page is the editor: click a line, type, done. The printed result is what you were looking at.

Two languages by default

Labels print as "INVOICE / 发票", "AMOUNT DUE / 应付金额", "Total / 合计". One document, readable on both sides of a cross-border deal.

Real PDF, selectable text

Printing uses the browser engine, so the PDF has text rather than a screenshot. Invoice numbers stay searchable and totals stay copyable.

Free and open source

MIT licensed, one HTML file, no server, no account. Fork it and change the layout to your taste.

Questions

Where is my data stored?

In this browser's local storage, on this device. Clear site data and it is gone, so keep a PDF of anything you need to keep.

Can I use it for quotes?

Switch the type to Quote and the heading changes to QUOTE / 报价单. The rest of the layout and the totals stay the same.

What about tax and discounts?

Set a rate and a discount as an amount or a percentage. Tax is calculated on the discounted subtotal, and the total updates while you type.

Why does printing need a dialog?

There is no server, so the browser's own PDF engine does the work. Choose "Save as PDF" and set margins to none for an exact A4 page.
